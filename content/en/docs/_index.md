Alexandria is a single-user personal library. It indexes the media and
documents already on your disk, records what it finds, and gives you one place
to browse, play, read, edit, and organise all of it — without re-encoding,
duplicating, or relocating a single file.

These pages introduce the project and point at the detailed specifications that
live in its code repositories.
